SPHEREx Blink

Enter a patch of sky and watch how SPHEREx's view of it changes over time. Moving dots are comets, asteroids, brown dwarfs or nearby stars.

What am I looking at? Stars that stay in place are far away. A dot that shifts between frames may be a nearby object, such as a comet, asteroid, brown dwarf or nearby star.

Frames play in time order. Brightness can change because each frame may show a different wavelength.
